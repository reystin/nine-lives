**Everyone has exactly one heart.** Nine Lives is a Fabric mod for Minecraft 26.3 that
turns a survival world into a shared hardcore run: max health is locked at 1 HP, lives
are earned with advancements, spent on death, and can be given to friends.

## Downloads

| File | Where to install |
|---|---|
| `ninelives-1.0.0+mc26.3.jar` | Server **and every player**, or just `mods/` in singleplayer. Full interface included. |
| `ninelives-server-1.0.0+mc26.3.jar` | **Server only.** Vanilla clients can join: Tab, chat, action bar and title feedback. |

Requirements: Minecraft **26.3**, Fabric Loader **0.20.1+**, Fabric API, Java **25**.
Mod Menu is optional for the client settings screen.

## Highlights

- 1 HP cap with working absorption, golden apples and totems.
- 9 starting lives; death spends one; totems save you for free.
- 15-second respawn protection, cancelled by attacking.
- Zero lives → spectator until a life is transferred.
- Vanilla mode: +1 life per 10 advancements and per curated hard achievement.
- Blaze and Caves mode: +1 per 50 BACAP advancements and per super challenge, with
  automatic datapack installation from Modrinth (SHA-1 verified, no bundled datapack).
- RU/EN dark glass interface, HUD, toasts, particles and sounds.

## Changelog

### 1.0.0 — first release

- 1 HP max-health cap with vanilla absorption, golden apples and totems.
- 9 starting lives; death spends one, totem saves do not.
- 15-second respawn protection, cancelled by attacking.
- Zero lives puts the player in spectator mode until a life is transferred.
- Life transfer UI; the giver always keeps at least one life.
- Vanilla advancement mode: +1 life per 10 advancements and per curated hard achievement.
- Blaze and Caves mode: +1 per 50 BACAP advancements and per super challenge.
- Automatic BACAP installation from Modrinth with SHA-1 verification, allow-listed
  domains, server-side download and no bundled datapack.
- Server-only build for vanilla clients (Tab, chat, action bar, title).
- RU/EN dark glass interface, HUD, toasts, particles and sounds.
