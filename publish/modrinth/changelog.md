## 1.0.0 — first release

- 1 HP max-health cap with vanilla absorption, golden apples and totems.
- 9 starting lives; death spends one, totem saves do not.
- 15-second respawn protection, cancelled by attacking.
- Zero lives puts the player in spectator mode until a life is transferred.
- Life transfer UI; the giver always keeps at least one life.
- Vanilla advancement mode: +1 life per 10 advancements and per curated hard achievement.
- Blaze and Caves mode: +1 per 50 BACAP advancements and per super challenge.
- Automatic BACAP installation from Modrinth with SHA-1 verification, allow-listed
  domains, server-side download and no bundled datapack.
- Server-only build for vanilla clients (Tab, chat, action bar, title).
- RU/EN dark glass interface, HUD, toasts, particles and sounds.
